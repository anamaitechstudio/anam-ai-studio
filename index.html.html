About the studio

Hi, I'm Anam — I build the documents that open doors.

I'm a Data Science student and self-taught digital services provider, running Anam AI Studio entirely from my phone. What started as helping classmates fix their CVs has grown into a small studio offering resume design, data entry, MS Office support and AI-assisted productivity work for students, job seekers and small businesses across Pakistan.

My background covers computer operations, MS Office, internet research, email management and the basics of Python and Java — alongside hands-on experience designing 100+ resumes for real clients. I care about getting the small details right: formatting that survives ATS screening, layouts that are easy to scan, and turnarounds that respect your deadline.
